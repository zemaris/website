Getting started

1. Install

  1. Run the setup file you downloaded. Its name starts with Velocity-Setup.
  2. If Windows shows a blue SmartScreen box, see this tip.
  3. Tick FFmpeg if you want MP3 and HD video, and keep "Start with Windows" ticked so Velocity is ready in the tray.

2. Connect your browser

  1. Open Start → Connect Velocity to Chrome.
  2. Follow the two clicks shown.
  3. A ⚡ Velocity icon appears in the browser toolbar.

3. Download

  1. Click any download link, or hover a video and click Download.
  2. Pick quality and folder in the info window.
  3. Click Start download.

Troubleshooting

A video won't download
  1. Wait a moment and try again. Velocity updates its video engine automatically, and a fix may already be on its way.
  2. Open Help → Check for updates to update now.
  3. For members-only videos, make sure you're signed in to the site in your browser.
  4. Still failing? Email us the page address.
The Download button doesn't appear on videos
  1. Check the ⚡ Velocity icon is in your browser's toolbar.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and make sure Velocity is switched on. Click the reload icon on it.
  3. Reload the video page.
Downloads are slower than expected
  1. Check no speed limit is set in Settings.
  2. Raise Connections to 16 or 32 in Settings.
  3. Some servers limit every user to a slow speed. Velocity can't go faster than the server allows.
The browser downloads files itself instead of Velocity

Make sure Velocity is running (look for its icon in the system tray). If it's closed, the extension starts it automatically the next time you download.

Windows SmartScreen shows a warning

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" for new apps it doesn't know well yet. It is a caution, not a sign of a virus. Click More info to see the publisher, then:

  • Our current installer is not digitally signed yet, so the publisher shows as Unknown publisher. That is expected for now. Before you click Run anyway, make sure you downloaded it from zemaris.com, and if you like, check the file.
  • Once our installers are signed, the publisher will show our name, and the warning goes away as more people install Velocity.
How do I check that my download is genuine?
  1. Open PowerShell (right-click Start → Terminal) and type Get-FileHash "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\Velocity-Setup-1.7.2.exe", using your file's name and folder.
  2. Compare the long code it shows with the SHA-256 on our Download page. They must be the same (capital or small letters don't matter).
  3. For a signed installer you can also right-click the file → Properties → Digital Signatures and check the name.
  4. If anything doesn't match, delete the file and email us.
MP3 or HD video fails to save

These need FFmpeg, a free program Velocity uses for audio and video. Setup offers it as a tick box; it is never installed without your choice. If you left it unticked, run setup again and tick FFmpeg, or email us for help.

Licence & activation

Activate in 20 seconds

  1. Copy the licence key from your purchase email. It starts with VEL-.
  2. In Velocity, open Help → Licence.
  3. Paste the key and click Activate.

Activate while you are online: Velocity registers the PC with our activation server, so your plan's PC limit can be kept. After that it confirms the key about once a week, and keeps working for up to 14 days without internet. Subscriptions renew by themselves: Velocity picks up the renewed key the next time it's online. Lost your key? Email us from the address you bought with.

Buying & refunds

Who takes my payment?

Our orders are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle takes the payment, adds the right tax for your country and sends your receipt. Your licence key comes from us, by email, straight away.

How do I cancel or change my card?

Use the link in your receipt email from Paddle, or look up your order at paddle.net. Velocity keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Ask within 14 days of buying for a full refund. See our refund policy.

I changed my PC. How do I move my key?

Enter your key on the new PC. If you've reached your plan's PC limit, email us and we'll free up a slot.

Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Velocity by Zemaris, and choose Uninstall. Your downloaded files are not removed.
