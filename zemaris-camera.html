Zemaris Camera

Writes the facts of a photo onto the photo: what, when, where, the weather and whose work it is. Made for site engineers, inspectors, surveyors and delivery crews.

Coming soon Android

Email me when ready All Zemaris software

On every photo

The details that make a photo useful as proof.

Time and place

A title, the date and time, GPS coordinates, the street address, altitude and compass heading.

Weather

Temperature and wind speed with gusts, in the units you choose.

Your company

Your company name and logo, in the stamp or as a watermark in any corner.

Your own lines

Project, client and worker names, serial numbers and any text you add. Gregorian or Hijri dates.

Made for site work

The whole crew stamps the same way.

Templates

Ready-made templates for site reports, construction sites, inspections and delivery proof, or make your own.

Share a template

Send a template file to your crew, logo included, so every phone stamps the same way.

Saved sites

Save a site with its address and lock it: the address is right on every photo, even without signal.

Older photos and video

Stamp photos you already took, and record stamped videos.

Photo log and reports

From photos to a report in minutes.

Photo log

Every photo is kept in a log with its time, place and notes.

PDF photo reports

A cover page, a photo index, 1, 2, 4 or 6 photos per page and a signature block.

Export and share

Export the log as Excel, CSV, KML or GeoJSON, and share photos with or without their location data.

Private by design

Your photos stay with you.

No account, no Zemaris server

Photos, videos and the photo log stay on your phone. Online extras (weather, street address, map, sync) stay off until you turn them on.

Four languages

In English, Urdu, Arabic and Hindi.

Before release

What we know so far.

System requirements

An Android phone with a camera and GPS. The full list comes with the release.

Not released yet

Zemaris Camera is still being tested on phones, so there is no download yet. Screenshots, the price and the download will be here at release. The features above may still change before then. The stamp camera is also part of Zemaris Scan.
