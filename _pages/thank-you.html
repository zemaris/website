Velocity Download Manager

  1. Open the email from Zemaris with your licence key (its subject starts with "Your Velocity") and copy the key. It starts with VEL-.
  2. In Velocity, open Help → Licence.
  3. Paste the key and click Activate.

Zemaris PDF

  1. Open the email from Zemaris with your licence key (its subject says "PDF licence key") and copy the key. It starts with VPDF-.
  2. Open Zemaris PDF and click File → Licence.
  3. Paste the whole key and click Activate.

Haven't installed Zemaris PDF yet? Get it here, then paste your key.
