Billing

Billing questions

How do I pay?

By card, PayPal or other local ways to pay, in a secure checkout run by Paddle.com, our online reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle takes the payment and handles taxes. We never see or store your card details. Your licence key is emailed to you straight away.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Velocity isn't right for you, ask within 14 days of buying for a full refund. See our refund policy.

What happens when a Monthly or Yearly plan renews?

It renews automatically. Velocity picks up the renewed key by itself the next time it's online, so there's nothing to paste. If you cancel, Velocity keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I cancel or change my card?

Use the link in your payment receipt email from Paddle, or look up your order at paddle.net. You can also email us and we'll help.

How do I move Velocity to a new PC?

Enter your key on the new PC. If you've reached your plan's PC limit, email us and we'll free up a slot.

Can I upgrade from Yearly to Lifetime?

Yes. Email us and we'll take what's left of your Yearly plan off the Lifetime price.

Do you offer student or bulk discounts?

Business pricing starts at 5 PCs. For schools and students, get in touch.
