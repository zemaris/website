Every Velocity feature

A download manager, video and music downloader and torrent client in one app. Here's everything it does, in plain words.

Full-speed engine

Built to use every bit of your connection.

Up to 32 connections per file

Each file is split into parts that download at the same time. 16 by default, up to 32.

Smart splitting

When one part finishes, its connection takes half of the biggest part still downloading, so no connection sits idle.

Slow-connection watchdog

Connections running far below the average are dropped and reopened automatically.

Connection reuse

Open connections are kept and reused, so there's no waiting to reconnect between parts.

Server-friendly limits

Velocity limits connections per site and backs off politely if a server asks, instead of failing.

Live progress window New in 1.7

A colour map of every connection, a 60-second speed chart, average and top speed, a per-connection table, a speed limit and "when finished" actions, for each download.

Crash-proof resume

Progress is saved constantly. After a crash, restart or power cut, downloads carry on from the last byte.

Automatic retries

Dropped connections and server hiccups are retried for you.

Speed limits

Cap total speed or a single download, so browsing and video calls stay smooth.

Privacy & safety

Your PC, your files, your business.

No account, no tracking

Velocity never asks you to sign up and doesn't track what you download.
