Speed engine

Many lanes, not one.
That's why it's faster.

Many servers limit how fast each single connection can go. Velocity opens many connections to the same file at once, and keeps every one of them busy until the last byte.

  • Up to 32 connections per file, with per-site limits so servers don't block you
  • Connections that finish early split the biggest remaining piece
  • Slow or stuck connections are dropped and reopened automatically
  • Connections are reused, so there's no wait to reconnect

FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the free trial work?

Install Velocity and every feature is unlocked for 30 days, with no card and no sign-up. When the trial ends, pick a plan and paste your licence key. Files you've already downloaded are always yours.

Does Velocity collect my data?

No. There's no account and no tracking. The browser extension talks only to Velocity on your own computer. Read our privacy policy.

Will it slow down my PC?

No. Velocity sits quietly in the system tray and uses very little memory while it waits. You can cap the speed so browsing stays smooth while it downloads.

Can I move my licence to a new PC?

Yes. Enter your key on the new PC. If you've reached your plan's PC limit, email us and we'll free up a slot.

Can I download anything from any site?

Velocity is a tool. Only download content you have the right to, such as your own uploads, Creative Commons media, or files the site lets you download. Respect copyright and each site's terms.
