FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the free trial work?

Install the app and every feature is unlocked for 30 days, with no card and no sign-up. When the trial ends, pick a plan and paste your licence key. Files you've downloaded or saved are always yours, and Velocity PDF still opens, reads and prints your files.

Do the apps collect my data?

No. There's no account and no tracking. Velocity Download Manager never sends what you download, and Velocity PDF never sends your documents. To check a licence key, an app sends only the key and an anonymous PC code to our activation server. Read our privacy policy.

Does one key work in both apps?

No. Each app has its own plans and its own licence key, so you only pay for the app you use.

Can I move my licence to a new PC?

Yes. Enter your key on the new PC. If you've reached your plan's PC limit, email us and we'll free up a slot.

Can I download anything from any site?

Velocity Download Manager is a tool. Only download content you have the right to, such as your own uploads, Creative Commons media, or files the site lets you download. Respect copyright and each site's terms.
