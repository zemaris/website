Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Zemaris apps and zemaris.com handle, why, and who else is involved. The short version: the apps run on your computer, have no accounts, show no ads and do not track you. Your downloads and your documents stay on your PC. We only get what we need to sell you a licence and help you.

The apps are the Velocity download manager (with its browser extension) and Velocity PDF (coming soon). The person responsible for your information ("we") is Muhammad Asif, trading as Zemaris, trading as Zemaris, Sultanate of Oman. Contact: admin@zemaris.com.

Velocity download manager

What stays on your computer

  • Your download list, settings and downloaded files are stored only on your PC.
  • The browser extension sends the links you choose to download, and the cookies needed to fetch them, only to the Velocity app on your own computer (address 127.0.0.1). They are never sent to us.
  • If you turn on the clipboard monitor, Velocity checks copied text for links on your PC. Clipboard contents are never sent anywhere.
  • To run the 30-day trial, Velocity saves the date you first started it on your PC (in its settings folder and in your Windows user registry).

What Velocity connects to, and what it sends

  • The sites you download from, to fetch the files you ask for.
  • zemaris.com, once a day, to check whether a new version of Velocity is out (it reads one small file). If there is one, Velocity downloads the installer from the address in that file. These requests contain nothing about you beyond what every web request includes, such as your IP address.
  • GitHub, once a day, to check for and download updates to the open-source video engine (yt-dlp).
  • Our activation server, when you activate a licence key, now and then while it is active (about once a week), when you move the key off a PC, and a few times near a subscription's renewal date to pick up the renewed key. It receives your licence key and an anonymous PC code: a one-way fingerprint made from your Windows installation's ID, so we can count PCs without knowing anything else about your computer. The server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers), which handles the requests for us and also sees your IP address.
  • Peers in the BitTorrent network, only when you download a torrent. Other peers can see your IP address while you share a torrent.
  • The Windows package manager (winget), only if you tick the FFmpeg box during setup. It downloads FFmpeg from its own publisher.

Velocity sends no usage statistics, no crash reports and no list of what you download.

Velocity PDF

Your documents are opened, edited and saved on your PC. Velocity PDF goes online only for the features below, and only when you use them:

  • AI assistant (optional, off until you set it up). If you choose an online AI service and enter your own key, the text of the document you ask about, your question and the assistant's tool results are sent to that service: Anthropic (Claude) or the OpenAI-compatible address you choose. That service handles them under its own privacy policy and your agreement with it. Translation uses the same AI service. If you choose a model running on your own PC (Ollama or LM Studio), nothing leaves your computer. We never see your key or your documents: your key is kept on your PC.
  • Text recognition in other languages. English is built in. The first time you use another language, its language data is downloaded from the jsDelivr network and kept on your PC.
  • Voice typing. The first time you use it, the speech engine is downloaded from GitHub and its speech model from Hugging Face. After that your voice is turned into text on your PC and is never sent anywhere.
  • Digital signatures. When you add a trusted timestamp, Velocity PDF sends a fingerprint (hash) of the signature, not the document, to a timestamp service (DigiCert, Sectigo, GlobalSign or FreeTSA). To add or check long-term validation data, it asks the certificate authorities named in the certificates for their revocation lists and status answers.
  • Making a PDF from a web page loads that page, like a browser does.
  • Connect AI apps (optional, off by default) lets AI apps on your own PC work with your open document through a private local connection. Nothing is sent to us.

Velocity PDF uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is part of Windows; Microsoft's Windows privacy settings apply to it. If you get Velocity PDF from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the purchase and download under the Microsoft Privacy Statement, and Microsoft may give us reports about installs and crashes as allowed by your Windows diagnostic settings. These reports do not include your documents.

When you buy

  • Purchases on zemaris.com are made with Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your payment details, name, email address, country and address details needed for tax, under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Paddle tells our activation server about your order: what you bought and Paddle's order, customer and subscription numbers. The server then asks Paddle for your name and email address, so it can send you the key. Our server stores your licence record (key, plan, number of PCs, end date, your name and email, those Paddle numbers, and the anonymous codes of the PCs that use the key) so it can send your key, renew it, and switch it off after a refund.
  • Your key is emailed to you through [EMAIL SENDING SERVICE], an email-sending service that delivers the message for us.
  • We use this information only to deliver and support your licence, prevent misuse of keys, and keep the records the law requires. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Our website

zemaris.com does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It counts page views and clicks on its main buttons to see how the site is used, without cookies and without personal data: only the day, the page, the button, the country, the site you came from and whether you use a phone, tablet or computer; your IP address is not stored, and browsers that ask sites not to track them are not counted. These counts are kept on our Cloudflare server and only we can see them. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers may keep standard logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security. The site remembers your light or dark choice in your own browser only. On the pricing page, the secure checkout window is loaded from Paddle only when you point at or click a Buy button; Paddle may use cookies needed for payment and to prevent fraud.

Email

If you write to us, or join the launch list, we use your email address and message only to reply or to tell you about the launch. Our mailbox is hosted by Zoho. We do not sell or share your address, and we'll remove you from the launch list whenever you ask.

How long we keep it

  • Licence records: for as long as the licence can be used, then for 2 years, so we can answer support questions and handle refunds and chargebacks.
  • Paddle keeps order and tax records for as long as tax law requires.
  • Emails: as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 2 years after our last message.

Where it is handled

Paddle, Cloudflare, the email services and GitHub work in many countries, so your information may be handled outside your own country. They protect it under their own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses where the law requires them.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it (unless we must keep it, for example for tax records or an open refund). You can also object to how we use it. Email admin@zemaris.com and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Children

Our apps and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For a big change that affects buyers, we will also email them.
