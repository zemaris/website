Zemaris Scan

Turns your phone into a document scanner, a text reader and a stamp camera. No ads, no watermarks, no account.

Coming soon Android

Email me when ready All Zemaris software

Scan

Clean scans of any page, fast.

Finds the page for you

Live edge detection, and the photo is taken by itself as soon as the page is steady and well lit.

Long documents

Scan page after page in one go, or import photos and PDFs you already have.

A mode for every paper

Documents, ID cards (front and back on one page), books, whiteboards, receipts, slides, photos, business cards and QR codes.

Filters and touch-ups

Auto, magic colour, grayscale, black and white and no shadow; crop with a magnifier, rotate, reorder, erase, sign and mark up.

Read the text

Your scans become text you can search and copy.

Text recognition on your phone

Works without internet, for Latin, Arabic, Urdu, Persian, Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese and Korean scripts.

Search and copy

Select and copy text on the page, search inside every document, and turn tables into CSV.

Share and export

The format you need, where you need it.

Searchable PDF

PDFs with a hidden text layer, and an optional AES-256 password.

Office and image files

JPG, PNG, TIFF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, TXT, RTF and ZIP.

Send it anywhere

Share, print, email, save to your files, or send to a computer on the same Wi-Fi.

Stamp camera

Photos that prove when and where.

Facts on the photo

Date and time, GPS position, street address, weather, your logo, and project and worker names on each photo.

Photo log

Every stamped photo is kept in a log you can export as CSV, Excel, a PDF report, KML or GeoJSON. Want only the stamp camera? See Zemaris Camera.

Private by design

Your documents stay with you.

No account, no Zemaris server

Your scans, their text and your library stay on your phone. Online extras stay off until you turn them on.

Locked documents

Lock documents with a PIN, fingerprint or face unlock.

Four languages

In English, Urdu, Arabic and Hindi.

Before release

What we know so far.

System requirements

An Android phone with a camera. The full list comes with the release.

Not released yet

Zemaris Scan is still being tested on phones, so there is no download yet. Screenshots, the price and the download will be here at release. The features above may still change before then.
