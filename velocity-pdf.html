Velocity PDF

Edit, sign, convert and protect PDFs on your own PC, with tools that are simple to find. Coming soon for Windows 11 and 10, in the Microsoft Store.

Coming soon

Join the launch list See the features

Edit like a document

  • Click a line or paragraph and type. Text stays in the document's own font, and the lines below move to make room
  • Change words in scans and pictures too
  • Edit, move and replace pictures
  • Find & replace across the whole file
  • Add text, pictures, shapes, stamps, watermarks, headers, footers, page and Bates numbers

Pages and files

  • Merge, split, reorder, turn, crop and extract pages
  • Compare two versions of a file
  • Make files smaller
  • Batch: do the same job on many files at once

Forms and signatures

  • Fill in any form; design new fields or edit existing ones
  • Sign with your own handwritten signature, saved for next time
  • Sign with a digital ID, with a trusted timestamp and long-term validation
  • Check the signatures in a file, in plain words

Convert

  • PDF to Word, Excel and PowerPoint you can edit, or to pictures and text
  • Create PDFs from Word, Excel and PowerPoint files (Microsoft Office needed), web pages and pictures
  • Text recognition (OCR) in 30+ languages, to make scans searchable

Protect

  • Passwords and permissions with AES-256
  • True redaction: what's under the box is removed from the file, and the rest of the page stays real text
  • Remove hidden information before you share

Accessible and archive-ready

  • Tags, alt text and reading order, so screen readers read pages in the right order
  • PDF/A files for long-term archiving
  • Read aloud

Drawings and plans

  • Measure distance, area and perimeter to scale
  • Show and hide layers

AI assistant, your choice

  • Optional: off until you set it up
  • Use your own key for an online AI service, or a private model running on your PC
  • Ask about a document, or let it make changes you can undo
  • Translate a whole document
  • Voice typing that runs on your PC
Private by design

Your documents stay on your PC

Files are opened, edited and saved on your computer. Velocity PDF goes online only for features you choose: an online AI service with your own key, extra text-recognition languages and the voice-typing model (downloaded once), and trusted timestamps for digital signatures. Read the details in our privacy policy.
