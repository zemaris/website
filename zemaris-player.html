Zemaris Player

Plays the videos and music on your PC and phone, in almost every format. No ads, no account, no tracking.

Coming soon Windows Android

Videos and music

One app for everything you play.

One library

Videos and music in one app, sorted by folder, artist and album.

Playlists

Make playlists, and import or export them as M3U files.

Open with

Open a video or song from another app or from your files straight in Zemaris Player.

Easy to control

Simple gestures and handy tools.

Gestures

Swipe to seek, change the volume and dim the picture; double-tap to jump back or forward.

Speed with natural sound

Play from 0.25x to 4x with pitch correction, so voices still sound right.

A-B repeat and sleep timer

Repeat a part over and over, or let playback stop by itself.

Subtitles and sound

Watch and listen the way you like.

Subtitles

SRT, WebVTT and ASS files, subtitles inside the video, and a timing shift when they run early or late.

Equaliser

A 10-band equaliser with presets.

On your phone and PC

Made for each system.

Android

Picture-in-picture, and music that keeps playing in the background with controls on the lock screen.

Windows

Your keyboard's media keys work, and the Windows media controls show what is playing, with its cover picture.

Before release

What we know so far.

System requirements

A 64-bit Windows PC, or a 64-bit Android phone. The full list comes with the release.

Not released yet

Zemaris Player is still being tested on phones and PCs, so there is no download yet. Screenshots, the price and the download will be here at release. The features above may still change before then.
